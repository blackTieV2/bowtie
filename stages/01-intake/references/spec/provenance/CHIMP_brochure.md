Vision
Your organization’s aspiration is to be higher than the ‘pathological’ ground-
level, it needs an effective system for tracking incidents, analyzing and trending

improvement.

One of the three Changer founders has also co-founded CGE Risk, ‘the leading

Introduction
CHIMP is a fully web based application that can manage your incidents, risk,
audits, reports etc.

One of the most effective way which would combine all your incidents into a
single platform for managing, auditing, figuring out the root causes, analyzing it
and taking actions based on priority.

Its proactive approach identifies the problem even before they likely to occur
and prevents from near incidents to happen.

The mobile enabled version helps to capture the real time incidents along with
a proof in the form of image, audio or video, day, date and time.
Incident reporting workflow
The incident portal allows organisations to create workflows from start to finish.
From initially reporting an incident, to analysing the underlying trends across
multiple incident reports.

Central web-based database
It creates an incident repository with consistent incident format, so the
organisation maintains unity across their incident reports.

Integration with incident analysis tools
It’s integrated with a Changer Audit tool (CAT) which allows you to create,
activate and manage all audits in your organization in a single database.

Reporting
Reporting leads to an improvement in safety performance. It identifies the root
causes of an incident and suggest a remedial action.
